Privacy Policy

Checklist app and website · Last updated November 17, 2026

Checklist keeps track of your assignments, notebooks, sticky notes and calendar events. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Everything you add is saved on your device first, so the app works without an internet connection.

Sync between your devices

To keep your devices in step, your checklist data (assignments and whether they're done, notebooks and their pages, sticky notes, calendar events, items in the trash, and your colour settings) is sent to our sync server, which runs on Cloudflare. It is stored under a sync code, not your name, email or any other identity, and is used only to show the same checklist on your devices. We never sell or share it, and we don't use it for advertising.

Reading a syllabus

If you choose to add a syllabus, the text, PDF or photos you pick are sent to our server so an AI model (Cloudflare Workers AI) can read the due dates out of them. The file is used only to answer that request and is not kept afterwards.

Camera, photos and microphone

The app only asks for these when you use a feature that needs them: the camera and photos to add a syllabus, and the microphone and speech recognition to dictate notes. Dictation is handled by Apple's speech recognition on your device or Apple's servers, under Apple's privacy policy.

Notifications

Due-date reminders are scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to a server to deliver them. You can turn them off in the app's ☰ menu or in iPhone Settings.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. To have your synced data removed from the server, contact us below.

Children

Checklist is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.
